Prediction Methodology & Mathematical Model:

Limitations: This transparent statistical forecast models normal business continuity. It does not account for unmodeled extreme weather events, civic disruptions, or sudden tariff overhauls.

Data Distribution & Tukey's IQR Outlier Analysis

Central tendency metrics (Mean, Median, Mode), Frequency Histogram, Box Plot Summary, and Interquartile Range (IQR) fence outlier detection.

Mean (Average) -- Arithmetic average
Median (50th %ile) -- Robust central value
Mode (Most Frequent) -- Modal frequency
Std Deviation (σ) -- Sample dispersion
IQR (Q3 - Q1) -- Interquartile range
Tukey Upper Fence -- Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

Syllabus Category Business Objective Active Excel Formula Formula Explanation
XLOOKUP Customer Name Retrieval =XLOOKUP(Bookings_Data!B2, Customers!A:A, Customers!B:B, "Guest User") Searches User_ID in Customers sheet; returns Name with native fallback for missing users.
VLOOKUP Facility Name by Location ID =VLOOKUP(Bookings_Data!C2, Locations!A:C, 3, FALSE) Extracts column index 3 (Facility Name) matching exact Location_ID.
SINGLE IF Settlement Classification =IF(Bookings_Data!O2="COMPLETED", "Settled & Verified", "Unsettled / Pending") Evaluates Status column; flags settled transactions vs pending sessions.
NESTED IF Occupancy Tier Rating =IF(Bookings_Data!N2>250, "Tier 1 (Surge)", IF(Bookings_Data!N2>100, "Tier 2 (Standard)", "Tier 3 (Short-Stay)")) Cascading logical thresholds classifying revenue yield into Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3.
AND + OR High-Yield Asset Identification =IF(AND(Bookings_Data!N2>150, OR(Bookings_Data!F2="car", Bookings_Data!F2="ev")), "★ High-Yield Asset", "Standard Dwell") Compound condition verifying both revenue > ₹150 AND vehicle is Car or EV.
IFERROR Lookup Error Protection =IFERROR(VLOOKUP(Bookings_Data!C2, Locations!A:C, 3, FALSE), "Location Not Found") Traps #N/A errors from invalid foreign keys, returning clean user-friendly text.
SUMIFS Multi-Condition Revenue Sum =SUMIFS(Bookings_Data!N:N, Bookings_Data!F:F, "car", Bookings_Data!O:O, "COMPLETED") Sums Total_Amount where Vehicle_Type is "car" AND Status is "COMPLETED".
COUNTIFS Cancelled Four-Wheelers =COUNTIFS(Bookings_Data!F:F, "car", Bookings_Data!O:O, "CANCELLED") Counts rows matching multiple criteria (Four-wheelers cancelled before entry).
IQR OUTLIER Statistical Outlier Flagging =IF(OR(Bookings_Data!I2>24, Bookings_Data!N2>1500), "🚨 STATISTICAL OUTLIER", "NORMAL") Flags duration > 24h or fare > ₹1,500 corresponding to Tukey Upper Fence.
